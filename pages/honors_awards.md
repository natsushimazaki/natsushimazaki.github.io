# 🏆 Honors & Awards
### `Sep 2024` **Member of First Place Winning Team** at [Datathon 2024](https://bigdatado.org/) 
A three-day Data Analytics and Data Science competition was hosted by the Dominican Data Science Society. There were two competitions: Data Visualization and Machine Learning. Our team won first place in the Data Visualization competition by developing a dashboard and data storytelling that highlighted patterns of fraudulent credit card transactions recorded by a Dominican financial institution.

### `July 2024` **Excellence in Graduate Mathematics** at [Shawnee State University](https://www.shawnee.edu/areas-study/college-arts-sciences/math-sciences/master-math) 
Recipient of the Excellence in Graduate Mathematics honors at the Evening of Honors – Mathematical Sciences event, held at Shawnee State University.

### `Jan 2020` **Robin L. Ryan Unsung Hero Award** at [Oregon State University](https://clubs.oregonstate.edu/math)
This award was "designed to celebrate a student who has been involved in a variety of activities and rarely receives recognition for their work “behind the scenes”. 

### `Sep 2013` **Member of First Place Winning Team** at [National Science Competition (Mi Viaje a NASA) 2013](https://www.educando.edu.do/articulos/estudiante/ciact-envia-estudiantes-la-nasa/)
- "Mi Viaje a NASA" competition is a Dominican national competition, organized by the Dominican presidency, promoting scientific research. Some of the subject matters include chemistry, biology, nanotechnology, engineering, robotics, etc. 
- The winner's prize consisted of a trip to a Space Center endorsed by National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) for an unforgettable and educational experience for 10 days. During that period, the participants, from all over the world, would engage in troubleshooting, extensive use of the scientific method, and teamwork.
- Awarded by the Presidency of the Dominican Republic.
